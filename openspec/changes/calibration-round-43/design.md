## Context

The assessment of 2026-11-17 on the repaired simulator (fingerprint d45a275dd7be0713cec6a64394b7d8a9, network f9fa09a5726644d7, demand e4f1993916827e82, cross-validation demand e480933c18b970f6) leaves the station rule failing: recent PM stations within GEH 5 are 78 of 92 fitted and 13 of 24 held out, AM 67 of 77 and 14 of 19. Several PM held-out misses are short blocks near Howell St and Canning St, where the model carries far more than the count (McAuley St Howell to Canning eastbound 161 against 12, 60th St Howell to Canning eastbound 130 against 23, Ayala Ave Martin to Howell northbound 155 against 60, 62nd St Colby to Canning eastbound 41 against 11). The project lead checked the stop control of the side streets against street-level imagery and decided the points below by card on 2026-11-18. Node control comes from OSM `highway=stop` nodes; a stop tag on a junction node stops every way that ends there or, when no way ends there, every approach.

## Decisions

D1. Project lead, 2026-11-18. Hillegass Ave at 61st St (OSM node 53085953) is an all-way stop. Street-level imagery with true compass headings (undated, appears to be from 2023 to 2025) shows STOP legends and stop signs on both Hillegass Ave approaches and on 61st St; sign detections in older imagery, seen 2019-12-22, agree. High confidence, DATA. The model had a two-way stop: 61st St ends at the junction, so the importer stopped 61st St and left Hillegass Ave free.

D2. Project lead, 2026-11-18. At Canning St and 62nd St (OSM node 53088342) the eastbound 62nd St approach, the west leg, has no stop; the other three approaches stop. The imagery gives a clear view of the west leg with no sign and no legend, and older sign detections show none on that corner. Medium confidence, so it is an ASSUMPTION pending a field check. The model had an all-way stop.

D3. Project lead, 2026-11-18. A survey of all 36 side-street junctions in the study area against the same imagery found no other mismatch at medium confidence or better. Every Howell St junction (59th St, McAuley St, 60th St, Ayala Ave) reads as uncontrolled, and so do Ayala Ave at Forest St and Ayala Ave at McAuley St; the model has all of them uncontrolled. Three lower-confidence items are open and are not changed: Dana St at North St (the model stops North St; no stop was seen), Canning St at 63rd St (a possible stop on 63rd St) and 63rd St at Dana St.

D4. Project lead, 2026-11-18. The imagery also shows "Road closed to thru traffic" barricades on Howell St at Ayala Ave, on Forest St at Ayala Ave and on Colby St at 61st St. The imagery is undated and the project lead does not know whether the barricades are still in place, so they are left out of the baseline.

D5. Mechanism. D1 uses the existing tag override: `stop=all` on node 53085953. D2 cannot be written as a tag correction, because the map puts the stop on the junction node and a node tag has no way to leave one approach out. The area config gets `stopExemptions`: a list of entries, each with the junction node, the way the approach arrives on, the direction of travel along the way's node order (`forward` or `backward`, as OSM's `direction` tag on a stop node uses them) and a source. The importer clears the stop of that approach after it has read the stop tags and before junctions are joined, so the node's control follows from the approaches that still stop (three of four: `two_way_stop`). An entry that matches no stopped approach, or that names a junction tagged `stop=all`, changes nothing and is listed in the import output. The mechanism is area data; the importer has no Rockridge-specific code. The network already has two junctions where three of four approaches stop, so the simulator needs no change. Way 6350365 (62nd St) runs east to west through the junction, so the eastbound approach is its `backward` direction.

D6. Keep rule, written before the assessment ran. The project lead's instruction is to update the area note and caveat only if the assessment is no worse than before on the station rule, stability and the whole-street counts, and otherwise to change nothing further and not to tune. Read strictly (ASSUMPTION about the wording): none of the four recent-station counts falls below 78 of 92, 13 of 24, 67 of 77 and 14 of 19; D16 holds in every period with no car cleared from gridlock in the judged zone, as before; all eight whole-street traversal counts stay within GEH 5. A fall of one station is within seed noise, but it still counts as worse here and is reported instead of published. The stop corrections themselves stay either way: they correct the network to the street and were not chosen for their effect on any count.

D7. The demand is rebuilt with `npm run data` on the new network. The frozen split is not re-frozen: no station changed, and the check in D8 confirms that no link id moved. The simulated membership files (`rockridge.through-membership.json`, `rockridge.station-membership-training.json`) come from runs on the old network and are used as they are. No fit setting is changed.

D8. Check on the network (step 1.3). Against the network of 2026-11-17, the rebuilt one differs in two nodes and three links and nothing else: node 53085953 `two_way_stop` to `all_way_stop` with `stopAtEnd` set on both Hillegass Ave approaches, node 53088342 `all_way_stop` to `two_way_stop` with `stopAtEnd` cleared on the eastbound 62nd St approach. Node and link counts and every id are unchanged. Network f9fa09a5726644d7 becomes 093cbd5469aad29c. A test on the real extract holds this.

D9. D10. Outcome (seeds 1 to 10, the assessment of 2026-11-17 in brackets). Demand 77ef92449de50092, cross-validation demand 09d3f3d769ead5ec, fingerprint unchanged, validation 67 of 74. D16 holds in every period with no judged clearing (holds). Recent stations within GEH 5: PM fitted 72 of 92 (78), PM held out 12 of 24 (13), AM fitted 69 of 77 (67), AM held out 15 of 19 (14). All eight whole-street counts stay within GEH 5; the Hillegass Ave traversals fall to 56 southbound and 40 northbound in the PM peak (81, 65). D11. Project lead, 2026-11-18, after D10: keep both corrections on the branch, refit the trip estimate on the corrected network with the membership iteration of rounds 39 to 42, and adopt the result only if it is no worse than the published assessment. Keep rule, written before any run of the refit. Against the published assessment of 2026-11-17 the refit is adopted only if none of these is worse: recent PM fitted stations within GEH 5 at least 78 of 92; PM held out at least 13 of 24; AM fitted at least 67 of 77; AM held out at least 14 of 19; D5 holds with 4 of 4 headline shares within 10 points; all 8 whole-street traversal counts within GEH 5; D16 holds in every period. One figure below its line fails the rule. The rule is not loosened after a result. Held-out counts inform no setting, the split stays frozen (be72380b485e), no fit knob is added, the simulator does not change and no station gets an adjustment of its own.

D12. The iteration of D11 was not run, because the procedure it names is not part of the published model and running it would have meant choosing a new one. What the check found:
- The measurement repair of 2026-11-17 made the demand build accept a membership file only when its evidence (network, split, config, stations, targets, count file, simulator, demand view) matches the build. The round-42 files carry no such evidence, so the published fit reports "lacks matching source, detector, split or demand-view evidence. Using static membership", and this round's fit reports the same. The statement in the first version of this round's record that the membership files were "those of the earlier network" was wrong and is corrected there: neither build uses them.
- The through targets that rounds 39 and 40 iterated on were retired in the same repair ("No comparable unique-corridor through counts enter this fit"). What is left of the mechanism is the list of sixteen round-42 blocks in `measuredStations`, chosen from a diagnosis on the earlier network and simulator.
- So the refit on the corrected network by the published procedure is `npm run data` with static membership, which is the build assessed in D10. Measured against the rule of D11 it fails: PM fitted 72 of 92, PM held out 12 of 24, D5 3 of 4.
- Simulated membership for the sixteen blocks on the new network would be a change the published model does not have, so a better score could come from the membership and not from the refit. Rounds 39 to 42 also give no single procedure to copy: round 39 pooled ten seeds over two iterations, round 40 forty and sixty runs, round 42 forty runs over two demands, each under guard rails frozen for that round, and none has a convergence rule.
The project lead decides whether to try it and, if so, with which seeds, pooling, number of iterations and comparison (design "What is not known").

## What is not known

- Whether the barricades of D4 are still in place. If they are, Howell St, Forest St and Colby St carry less through traffic than the baseline allows, which bears on the held-out misses near Howell St. Open question for the project lead; a field check or dated imagery would settle it.
- The date of the imagery behind D1 and D2. D2 rests on not seeing a sign, which is weaker evidence than seeing one.
- The three open items of D3.
- Whether the two corrections stay in the baseline, given D10 and D12. If simulated membership is to be brought back for the sixteen round-42 blocks, the project lead sets the procedure first: PM and AM seeds, whether runs of more than one demand are pooled, how many iterations, and whether the published network gets the same treatment so the two can be compared like for like.
