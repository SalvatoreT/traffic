## Context

Rounds 33 to 35 changed the phasing, the timing and the eastbound lanes at Ashby Ave / Claremont Ave. None of them reduced the cars cleared from gridlock at the junction or on the hillside streets behind it, and the delay-feedback fit stays rejected on that gridlock (10 to 16 cars an hour at iterations 2 and 3). The lead's reading on 2026-11-16: two northbound lanes with even 17 s of green in 100 s discharge about 600 cars an hour, while the model passes 260 to 370 and clears cars from gridlock there, so something other than the signal limits the junction. The lead asked for that constraint to be found before any further change to the phasing, then for each fix at its source to be measured one at a time, keeping it only if cars cleared from gridlock get no worse on the shipped, iteration-2 and iteration-3 demand on seeds 1 to 10 and 11 to 20; and, if the constraint is real or cannot be settled without field data, to stop and name the observation that would settle it. The numbers are in round 36 of `data/calibration/rockridge.record.md`.

## Decisions

D1. Reproduction. `npm run data` in the round's worktree reproduced network f9fa09a5726644d7 and demand 1c798217e72fb1ba, round 35's files byte for byte. PM seed 1 on them matches round 35's stored run (the probe of D2 attached) in every simulated field.

D2. Probe. A read-only probe in `calibrate` (`--probe`, `--probe-links`; a `probe_lanes` accessor on the simulation) writes, every 0.25 s step after the warm-up, one row per lane of 26 links around the junction: signal state, standing vehicles and queue length, and the head vehicle's next link, turn, distance to the line, speed, permission and what held it (downstream space, a vehicle crossing the junction, a pedestrian, or a priority rule). Discharges are counted when a head within 15 m of the line leaves its lane. The run is unchanged by it (PM seed 1 identical). It was not committed: it lives in the simulator's module tree, so landing it would change the fingerprint f0df1aabc323 on which the published proposals were searched and confirmed. The patch is kept with the round's scratch output.

D3. The chain. On round 33's iteration-2 demand (PM, seeds 1 to 4, 12.3 cars cleared from gridlock an hour; shipped demand in brackets, 1.0):
- Claremont Ave northbound (link 4329) is green 814 s an hour (720); its two lanes are queued at the line for 445 and 656 s of it (239 and 393). While green and queued its two lanes discharge 0.17 and 0.14 vehicles a second (0.42 and 0.28), against 0.42 to 0.56 at 1,500 to 2,000 vehicles an hour per lane [FHWA-SIG2004]. The head is held by a full downstream link 70% and 75% of that time (32% and 52%): the through lane by link 3446 (43 m to Claremont Blvd), the right-turn lane by link 3425 (Ashby Ave eastbound, 37 m to the slip-lane junction).
- Link 3446 is held 50 to 61% of its queued time by link 3431, Claremont Ave northbound beyond Claremont Blvd: one lane (OSM way 201989355, `lanes=2`), 82 m to an all-way stop at Russell St. Link 3431 is queued 3,390 s an hour (3,080) and discharges 340 cars an hour (345) at 0.10 a second. All four approaches of that stop are queued most of the hour (3,390, 3,560, 3,370 and 3,140 s; 3,080, 3,520, 3,380 and 1,810 shipped) and pass about 1,370 cars an hour together.
- Link 3425's inner lane is held by 250 cars an hour (175) that turn left across westbound Ashby Ave into the westbound right-turn slip lane (link 3449), a 134 degree turn. The slip lane carries 550 an hour (230) and is held 52% (55%) by full links ahead of it, mostly 3431 again. These cars are Claremont Ave northbound through traffic that turns right on red, then left into the slip lane, and Ashby Ave eastbound left-turners.
- Ashby Ave eastbound beyond the slip lane (link 4397) merges two lanes into Tunnel Rd's one eastbound lane at the Ashby Ave / Domingo Ave / Tunnel Rd signal, where Domingo Ave gets 1,770 s of green an hour (1,710) and Ashby Ave 1,350 (1,410). Domingo Ave southbound carries 500 cars an hour (560), 410 (500) of them turning left onto Tunnel Rd. Of those left turns about 220 an hour come down Claremont Ave from the north edge and turn left at the Russell St stop, and about 90 come along Russell St from Claremont Blvd: a route from the north hills to Tunnel Rd that avoids Ashby Ave / Claremont Ave and loads both the all-way stop and the Domingo Ave phase.
- Ashby Ave eastbound into the junction (link 3395) is held by full downstream links 63% of its queued green (21% shipped), and the queue reaches back over Claremont Crescent (link 3045 held 85% of its queued time), where the hillside gridlock starts.
So the northbound approach does not reach saturation flow because the one-lane Claremont Ave beyond it ends 125 m on at an all-way stop that runs at capacity, and part of that stop's load is the hillside traffic's route to Tunnel Rd through Russell St and Domingo Ave.

D4. Sources along the chain. OSM: Claremont Ave north of Claremont Blvd is one lane each way (ways 201989355 and 201989356, `lanes=2`), as the network has it. The Russell St junction carries `highway=stop` on the junction node (node 53082604) with neither `stop=all` nor a direction; the importer reads that as an all-way stop. The OSM wiki page for the tag [OSMStop] says minor-road stops are mapped on the approaches, not on the junction node, and all-way stops on the node with `stop=all`, so the tag as mapped is ambiguous. The extract has 172 such junction-node stops without `stop` or direction, so no general reading was changed. The City of Berkeley's 2026 Claremont quick-build presentation [BerkeleyClaremontQB2026] lists, at Claremont Ave and Russell St, added advance yield markings and "Yield to Ped" signs, which are used where approaching traffic is not stopped, and its 2025 slides [BerkeleyClaremontQB2025] give Claremont Ave peak-hour volumes of about 600 northbound (15:30 to 16:30) and 400 southbound (17:15 to 18:15) from data collected on 2025-09-23 at three locations along the project corridor (Claremont Ave from Russell St to the southern city limit). Neither document states the control at Russell St. [SafewayCollege-DEIR2011] does not study Russell St or the Domingo Ave / Tunnel Rd signal; it records that eastbound queues at Ashby Ave / Claremont Ave did not clear in the 2010 PM peak and gives 697 vehicles an hour leaving northward (359 through, 99 eastbound left, 239 westbound right). The Caltrans 2025 Bay Area bike plan needs list [CaltransD4BikeNeeds2025] (item Ala-13-NX04) names the slip lane at Claremont Ave; no source says whether eastbound Ashby Ave traffic can enter it. Found in passing: OSM restriction 14485297 (no left from Tunnel Rd westbound onto Domingo Ave, weekdays 16:00 to 18:00) does not reach the network because its `to` way is absorbed by junction joining, and Ashby Ave eastbound's right-only lane at Domingo Ave (`turn:lanes:forward=none|right`) is used by through traffic, since the importer reads only left-turn lanes.

D5. Candidate A, Claremont Ave / Russell St as a minor-road stop (Russell St stops, Claremont Ave does not), scratch network, PM seeds 1 to 4: cars cleared from gridlock 39.8 an hour on the iteration-2 demand against 12.3, and 9.3 against 1.0 on the shipped demand. Claremont Ave north of the junction carries 790 to 830 an hour instead of about 340, and the gridlock moves to Avalon Ave at Claremont Blvd, Russell St and Oak Knoll Ter, whose stop-controlled approaches cannot enter the heavier Claremont Ave and Claremont Blvd streams. Not kept; not measured on the other seed sets, since it already fails the step rule.

D6. Candidate B, bans on the eastbound left into the slip lane (3425 to 3449) and on the U-turn from the slip lane back onto Claremont Ave southbound (3449 to 3447), scratch network, seeds 1 to 4: 13.0 against 12.3 (iteration 2) and 1.5 against 1.0 (shipped); with A as well, 16.0 and 16.3. Not kept: no gain, and no source says the movements are barred.

D7. Stop. The all-way stop meters traffic that the hillside streets cannot absorb otherwise, so removing it moves the gridlock instead of clearing it, and the share of its load that is the Russell St / Domingo Ave route has no count. Whether the junction's real constraint is this stop cannot be settled without field data. Following the lead's instruction, the round stops here: no network or demand change, no new fit (round 35's replay stands: every iteration rejected), no new assessment. Every published file is round 35's.

D8. The observation that would settle it: a weekday PM peak-hour turning-movement count at Claremont Ave / Russell St, noting which approaches have stop signs. It gives the control, the Claremont Ave northbound volume the stop must pass, and the southbound left turns onto Russell St that feed the route to Tunnel Rd. A count of Domingo Ave southbound left turns onto Tunnel Rd at Ashby Ave would be the second most useful.
