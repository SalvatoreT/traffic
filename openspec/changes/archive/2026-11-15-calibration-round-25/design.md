## Decisions

D1. Colby St northbound is a routing fault, not a volume fault. The through intents add up to 101 veh/h against 102 whole-street cars counted and the exits at Alcatraz Ave were right. The trails show 140 cars per run entering at the south end (the count has 182 per hour), 106 joining from side streets (count: about 62) and 29.5 through cars joining from 63rd St and the other side streets: the cheapest way for a car from Telegraph Ave or the ramps to the Alcatraz Ave turn is often by the north end. An intent can now require the whole-street route of the volunteer count (`wholeStreet`); only seed flows whose route to the movement enters at the first link are candidates. The volumes are unchanged by this rule.

D2. Through intents at 0.47 and 0.15. With the whole-street rule alone the freeway share of Colby St northbound fell to 37.2% (0.2 inside the 10-point limit). The street's freeway share is 0.47 of its trips. Round 21 took 65 of the 102 whole-street cars as freeway-origin (0.40 of the exits) after a mid-street correction; the upper end of its range takes every freeway-origin trip of the street to be a whole-street car (77 of 163 exits, 0.47 for the left and the right turn). The south-side intent takes the rest of the through share (0.15) and the local intent stays at 0.38, so the intents add up to the counted exits and the whole-street count. ASSUMPTION; the evidence is the count's freeway share and its whole-street count. Result: freeway share 42.7%, whole-street cars 95.

D3. The costs do not explain the off-ramp split. The model's delays are below the Synchro worksheets [SafewayCollege-DEIR2011] [CHRCO-EIR2014] on both paths, by more on the 52nd St path (20 to 38 s at Telegraph Ave and 51st St, 6.5 s and 21 s at Shattuck Ave and 52nd St) than on the Clifton St path (5 to 7 s at Clifton St, 5 to 16 s at Hudson St). Matching the worksheets would favor the Clifton St path further. The 2010 count at the Clifton St terminal has 442 of 617 cars turning north and 67 south; the model sent 193 south, half of the south Oakland trips of the ramp. The destination mix of the trips is therefore wrong in the simulator's ramp choice, while the static fit already agrees with the counts (63 south, 1,045 and 752 on the two ramps).

D4. Ramp choice upstream. The builder divides each off-ramp flow over the ramps its final static assignment uses and gives each part the ramp movement as its `via`, with `maxDetour` 1.3 and the other ramps as alternatives, reusing the assumed-movement machinery of the simulator (rounds 20, 22 and 23). Volumes do not change. ASSUMPTION: the split of the fit stands in for drivers' ramp choice, which the data do not give. Consequence: a scenario that makes one ramp much dearer moves its trips only when the detour test fails. Only off-ramps; the on-ramp choice stays with the simulator (the headline shares at on-ramps hold without it).

D5. Ramp counts keep full weight (`exemptRamps`). The 2010 and 2013 counts and the 2012 Caltrans rows are the only evidence of the Claremont Ave ramp split, so the recency weight of round 24 (0.5 beyond 10 years) removed their anchor: the static fit had 1,045 and 752 on the two ramps and now has 1,015 and 631 (counted 1,024 and 617). ASSUMPTION; recorded for the owner as a question the record answers: ramp counts should be exempt.

D6. The older counts: the seed table is not the cause. 66% of the PM through flows and 55% of the local flows sit at the lower bound (69% and 61% of their seed volume), but the through table is cut on the hills pairs and the local table is raised by 40%. The static assignment passes 83% of the older stations and the simulator 50%; the simulator carries 0.84 of the static volume on the west corridors. Turning movements and approach totals fail alike (53% and 53%). Lower bound 0.05 and local trip rates at 60% did not raise the older counts (the second broke D5 and the station rule). No growth rate was added (round 24: no verified rate). Left for a feedback loop on volumes.

D7. Status. D16 holds in all periods, D5 holds, the through cars are inside their ranges and the station rule on recent counts holds, fitted and held out, in all periods. `status` stays `calibrated`; the calibration note and caveat give the new numbers and the remaining mismatches.
