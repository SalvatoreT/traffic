## Decisions

- **Width.** `--page-max: 1760px`. Views pad themselves with `max(gutter, (100cqw - cap) / 2)`, so the page is centered but sticky bands (the Assumptions section headings) still reach both screen edges. `.stage` is a size container for `cqw`.
- **Card arrangements by container query.** The card is a container named `card`. Narrow under 44rem, medium from 44rem (map | readout and lists), wide from 76rem (map | readout and streets | filters and key). Breakpoints follow the card, not the viewport, so they hold wherever the card sits. The street table keeps its stacked rows in any column narrower than 38rem.
- **Sticky.** From medium up, and only when the viewport is at least 640 px tall, the map column and the readout are `position: sticky` in their own columns. They sit beside the lists, so they cover no content. The map width is capped by `100dvh` so the map and its color ramp fit. `--pm-pin` is 1 while sticky is on; script reads it to decide whether the map is already kept in view. `scroll-padding-top` on the stage keeps a keyboard-focused row from landing under the sticky readout (WCAG 2.4.11).
- **Tooltip is `aria-hidden`.** The readout region already carries the same text and is `aria-live`; a `role=tooltip` tied to a focused element would announce it twice, and the focused element is a list row, not the map mark. The tooltip is visual support. It is anchored to the pointer's entry point (mouse, tap) or the street's midpoint or the change's mark (keyboard, list hover), so it does not follow the pointer and can be hovered (WCAG 1.4.13). Escape clears it from anywhere on the page. It is clamped to the visible part of the map and holds no focusable content.
- **Map marks stay out of the tab order.** The lists are the keyboard equivalent; making 60 streets tab stops would bury the page.
- **Focus pins only for keyboard focus.** Pointer focus used to pin and the click then unpinned, so a tap on a phone did nothing. A row pins on `:focus-visible` focus; a click pins and, when the map is not in view and not sticky, scrolls to it (instant under reduced motion) and shows "Back to list" in the readout.
- **Narrow cards and short screens** show a bar stuck to the bottom of the card with the tooltip text and "Show on map" while a selection exists and less than half the map is in view.
- Figures (through cars, residents' detour, all cars) sit in one row above the map, side by side from 44rem.
