## Context

After round 33 the delay-feedback fit converged with through cars and headline shares in or near range but was rejected on gridlock, which sat at Ashby Ave / Claremont Ave and the hillside streets behind it. Round 33 had modeled a resident field observation (a left-turn arrow for northbound Claremont Ave) as a protected phase added to a flow-based plan. The lead's reading: the observation and the 2010 PM worksheet of [SafewayCollege-DEIR2011] describe one plan, split phasing of the two Claremont Ave approaches, which round 33 tried only with the queue-first left-turn model. The lead asked for that plan on the default engine, then the queue-first model, then the fit, each step kept only if validation and the network both improve or stay equal. The numbers are in round 34 of `data/calibration/rockridge.record.md`.

## Decisions

D1. The worksheet plan. PDF page 482 (re-read from the stored copy): cycle 100 s; Claremont Ave northbound and southbound split (protected phases 8 and 7), greens 17.0 s and 17.5 s, clearance 4.5 s each; Ashby Ave eastbound and westbound in one phase (2 and 6, permitted lefts), 50.5 s, clearance 6.0 s. Greens plus clearances equal the cycle. Northbound is one shared lane group of two lanes; southbound has a left-turn lane beside a shared group, which matches OSM `left|left;through|through;right`. Ashby Ave eastbound is coded with two lanes; OSM has one (way 34361024, checked 2024). OSM lanes were kept for Ashby Ave because a lane change would be a separate decision with no current evidence.

D2. Per-phase clearance. The engine took one clearance per observed timing, so the worksheet's 6.0 s and 4.5 s could not both hold. An observed approach may now carry its own clearance; the phase that holds it uses it, the others the timing's or `YELLOW + ALL_RED`. The plan check is unchanged in kind: greens plus clearances must equal the cycle or the plan is an issue. The importer keeps the first observation's clearance on the timing and writes a differing one on the approach's green. Arithmetic is exact for the existing uniform plans (sums of 4.0 and 5.0 s), so existing plans are bit-identical.

D3. Lane coding. An observation may set `noLeftTurnLane`; the importer marks the approach links `noPocket` and the simulator adds no class-rule pocket there. A general rule (no pocket wherever OSM turn lanes show a shared left and no left-only lane) would have touched about seventy ways and was not built: this round is narrow.

D4. Provenance. PM: DATA (2010), consistent with the field observation. AM and midday: ASSUMPTION, the PM plan reused. That follows the area config's convention for observed plans without a worksheet for the period (52nd St at Shattuck Ave midday reuses the PM worksheet; Colby St at Claremont Ave PM reuses the AM count), rather than flow-based splits on the same phase structure.

D5. Step 1 reverted. PM cars cleared from gridlock per hour, seeds 1-10 / 11-20, round 33 plan against worksheet plan: shipped 1.1 / 1.3 against 1.7 / 2.1; iteration 2 13.9 / 13.6 against 11.3 / 14.0; iteration 3 10.6 / 16.0 against 16.1 / 18.3. Claremont Ave northbound carries less (shipped 373 to 333 veh/h; iteration 3 257 to 217) and its queue reaches the hillside streets; Ashby Ave eastbound gains a little (shipped 474 to 528). Keeping the class-rule pocket (diagnostic) gave 1.3 and 13.3 on seeds 1-10, so the lane coding is not the main cost. The network got worse, so the area config keeps round 33's observation and the network file is round 33's. The engine and importer support (D2, D3) stays because it changes no run without the new fields and lets a future timing sheet be entered as written.

D6. Queue-first left turns stay off. On the worksheet network, `--queue-first-left` gave shipped 1.3 / 1.5 against 1.7 / 2.1 but iteration 2 16.3 / 21.3 against 11.3 / 14.0 and iteration 3 16.0 / 19.7 against 16.1 / 18.3. It gets worse on the iteration demands, which fails the lead's condition. On round 33's plan round 33 already measured it worse.

D7. Fit. With step 1 reverted the network, demand and runs equal round 33's (seed 1 of each period, PM seeds 11-20 and the rebuilt iteration-2 seed 1 checked bit for bit with the new binary). `tools/cost-loop.ts --no-stop --resume 3` replayed the stored iterations under the unchanged rule: converged at iteration 3 (gap 0.090), every iteration rejected, shipped demand rebuilt byte for byte. Rerunning the simulations would have reproduced the same numbers.

D8. `float_roundtrip`. serde_json parses floats exactly with this feature, so a report that `calibrate --seeds` reads and rewrites keeps every digit; the solo seed-1 report and the `--seeds` copy are byte-identical. Simulation numbers do not change (PM seed 1 identical). Two golden strings in `tests.rs` that pass metrics through a JSON parse moved in their last digit. The simulator fingerprint does not hash Cargo features, so this change alone would not have moved it; the fingerprint changed this round because of D2 and D3.

D9. Verdict. Status stays `uncalibrated`; every figure equals round 33's. The area note was shortened to the test's 160 characters and the caveat says the worksheet plan was tested and not used, in visitor wording ("cars cleared from gridlock").
