## Decisions

D1. Lead-lag in observed signal timing. The worksheet of 52nd St and Shattuck Ave [CHRCO-EIR2014] lists eastbound left 33.5 s, eastbound through 57.2 s, westbound left 3.9 s, westbound through 27.6 s, northbound 34.9 s and southbound 25.4 s in a 110 s cycle (AM: 90 s, 37.7 s and 19.4 s). Round 17 gave each of the two 52nd St approaches a phase of its own, which cost the eastbound through 24 s of its green. An observation now may carry `runsWithHeading`; the importer lists that approach's links as `runsWith`. The plan gives the approach a phase alone of `greenSeconds` less the partner's green (29.6 s) and adds it to the partner's phase (27.6 s), so the eastbound through has 57.2 s and the cross street takes the rest (37.8 s). A link in two phases takes the better signal state. The protected left is shown as the permissive green of the shared phase (ASSUMPTION). Midday keeps the PM plan (ASSUMPTION of round 17). Result: eastbound approach 833 to 1,110 veh/h, 52nd St ramp 682 to 899, PM removals 1.10 to 0.40 on seeds 1 to 10 and 0.75 to 0.35 on 20 seeds, AM removals 0.10 to 0.00.

D2. Removal sites over seeds 1 to 20 of the round 22 state (15 removals): 10 at the 52nd St approach to Shattuck Ave and the Shattuck Ave northbound approach (D1), 3 on Claremont Crescent, 1 at Alcatraz Ave and Shattuck Ave, 1 on a service link near the Ashby Ave and Tunnel Rd gate. The Crescent joins Ashby Ave by a 9.6 m one-way link behind an OSM stop sign and its last car waits to merge into the Ashby Ave eastbound queue of the Claremont Ave signal. The network gives that approach one lane (OSM `lanes=2` for both directions) and the Safeway worksheet gives a saturation flow of two lanes; no source says where a second lane starts, so nothing was changed. Four of the seven remaining removals are there.

D3. Alternatives match on the pair. `withAlternatives` lists, for a flow, the other movements that have an intent whose origin set and destination set contain the flow's origin group and destination group. The 10 flows of 66 veh/h that had none now have one (455 of 455). Owner-visible consequence: in the owner's scenario those trips make the parallel street's turn-in and turn west at the diverter, which raises 63rd St by about 40 veh/h (1,123 to 1,165 for all blocks, three seeds). They do not loop.

D4. Off-ramp split. All ramp trips start at the SR-24 eastbound gate. The destination groups that tip the split are south Oakland and the Berkeley side streets, whose two paths cost the same to within 1.4 s free-flow; the Clifton St path was 32.5 s cheaper loaded before D1 and 19.1 s after. No defect was found at Clifton St (ramp approach delay 7.2 s in the worksheet [SafewayCollege-DEIR2011], 9 to 12 s in the model). The static fit puts 718 on the Clifton St ramp (held out, count 617), against 828 simulated. No change beyond D1.

D5. Observed plans for more signals were tried and not kept. Fourteen signals (17 observations) from [SafewayCollege-DEIR2011] and [CHRCO-EIR2014], in three groups, each on top of D1. The Telegraph Ave group takes Telegraph Ave SB to 286 s (observed 300 to 376). The College Ave group raises through cars on Hillegass Ave SB and Colby St SB to 119 and 113 for no station gain. The Claremont Ave group lowers removals by 2 in 10 runs and raises Hillegass Ave SB through cars by 6. Station differences between variants (129 to 133 of 190 fitted) are seed noise.

D6. Stations. 84 of 245 PM stations fail (64 low, 20 high); 56 are intersection-count stations (100 stations, 44 pass, model at 0.82 of the counts). D1 recovers 4 fitted stations. The rest sits in the demand fit (median factor of counted flows at the lower bound 0.10, counts of 2010 and 2013 used without growth) and is left for a change to the fit. Two defect candidates were found and not changed: the Ashby Ave and Claremont Ave slip lane (link 3449) is absent from the count's leg stations and the router allows a sharp left turn onto it.

D7. Status. D16 and D5 hold in all periods; the station rule fails (PM 69% fitted, 62% held out; AM 67%, 70%; midday 77%, 79%). `status` stays `uncalibrated`, `experimentalPeriods` stays `["pm_peak"]`.
