## Why

The simulator's router charges the control delay of a junction twice. A link's cost is the larger of its free-flow time and a moving average of the times measured on it (`update_costs`, 60 s epochs). The measured time already holds the queue, signal and stop wait. The cost of the movement at the link's end then adds a constant from `crates/sim/data/node-delays.json` (signal 10 s, all-way stop 8 s, minor stop 14 s, circle 4 s) in `eff.rs`, and `apply_mv_times` adds the movement's measured excess on top. Once a link has been measured, the constant is paid again.

The charges are not the same on every route. The arterial route from Berkeley and north Oakland to the SR-24 ramps passes four signal approaches. The residential diagonal (Telegraph Ave, McAuley St or 60th St, Howell St or Ayala Ave, Forest St, Claremont Ave) passes one stop and one signal. The double charge therefore favors the diagonal, where the simulator carries about twice the counted traffic: the five diagonal stations in the PM peak count 178 veh/h and the simulator carries 349 on the shipped demand and 453 on the training view. The calibration record's "Router diagnosis for the diagonal" entry (single seed, cloud container, scratch branch `router-diagnosis`) found 901 at those stations on its seed and 767 with the constant charged once, with Colby St NB at 0.74 of its count (0.71 before) and the fitted pass count 72 (70 before).

The archived design of change `ramp-and-colby-calibration` says the double count "is the same on every route and was left as it is". By the count above it is not. The references the router cites cost routes on experienced time ([Wardrop1952], [Sheffi1985], [Peeta2001]), and the demand fit's specification subtracts control delay so that queues count once. `openspec/specs/traffic-simulation/spec.md` states the two rules (the constants in one file; a movement's cost) without saying how they combine.

## What changes

- A router option, `Config::single_control_charge`, off in the published model. On, a link's cost is `max(free flow + control delay at its end, measured time)` and a movement's cost drops the constant: the constant is a floor on links that are unmeasured or lightly loaded and drops out once the measured time exceeds free flow plus the constant. Initial placement (`initial_times`) keeps the static prior. The default is the constant `params::SINGLE_CONTROL_CHARGE` (false). `calibrate --single-control-charge` and `--no-single-control-charge` select it, as `--queue-first-left` selects its option. With the option off the output is bit-identical to `origin/derived-targets-not-judged` (design D1).
- Rust unit tests of the cost function in both modes: unmeasured, lightly loaded and congested link, the movement's measured excess, and a route choice that differs.
- Stage 1, a cheap laptop diagnostic with the published demand and the cross-validation demand of the derived-targets job: `calibrate` PM and AM, seeds 1 to 10, option off and on, scored under the judged scope of `derived-targets-not-judged`. The bar to proceed is written in design D2 before any run.
- Stage 2, the full round, only if stage 1 meets the bar: adopt the option in code, rebuild demand with the membership loop on the new router, ten-seed assessment, fresh-seed confirmation, validation suite, and only if the keep rule passes, the proposal search, redistribution and results, all staged and nothing published.
- A spec delta that reconciles the two requirements for both settings of the option.
- A runbook and zsh scripts for the laptop; evidence returns on the development branch in gzip parts.

## Impact

Simulator source changes, so the fingerprint changes (design D1 gives the old and new values). Nothing published changes: the option is off, the shipped demand, network, proposals, results and redistribution files stay as they are, and every published number is reproduced by the new build. If stage 1 fails the bar, the option stays in the code, off, and the evidence is the result. If stage 2 passes, adoption flips `SINGLE_CONTROL_CHARGE`, which needs the restamp of "When the simulator fingerprint changes" in `data/README.md`, a new membership loop and a new proposal search; it is the project lead's decision.
