## i1-shipped-build (rockridge.fit.md)


In a period whose stations take simulated membership, the fit first runs as the baseline does (static membership, no ceilings). Each gate direction that no gate count holds in the period (no gate station in the fit names it) then has a one-sided ceiling: its car total in that static fit plus 0%, weight 10. The ceiling acts only while the gate's assigned total is above it, reads no count value, and does not change how flows that touch no count are treated. After the fit (median rule and guard cut included) a hard projection scales the flows of every ceiling gate direction still more than 0.1% above its ceiling down to it, so the ceilings hold on the final flows; the cost to the count fit is the change in fitted stations within their tolerance. A regularizer toward the static fit; the tolerance and the weight are assumptions, not data. Excess = final total above the ceiling (more than 0.5 veh/h). Periods without simulated membership have no ceilings.
