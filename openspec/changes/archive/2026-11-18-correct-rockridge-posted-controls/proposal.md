## Why

Oakland posts 30 mph on Claremont Avenue between Hillegass and College. The Rockridge network assigns 25 mph across those two blocks. At 53rd and Gaskill, the city installed an all-way stop in November 2022. The model lets traffic enter from all three approaches without stopping.

## What changes

- Allow a study area to set a sourced speed limit on part of one OSM way, in both directions.
- Apply the posted limit on Claremont between the two intersections. Use the existing node tag override to mark the all-way stop at 53rd and Gaskill.

## Impact

The corrections affect the network used for the Rockridge baseline and scenarios. Older results retain the network on which they ran.
