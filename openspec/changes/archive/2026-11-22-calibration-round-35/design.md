## Context

After round 34 the delay-feedback fit converged with the four through-car blocks and the headline shares in or near range, but the acceptance rule rejected it on gridlock (10.6 cars cleared an hour at iteration 3) at Ashby Ave / Claremont Ave and on the hillside streets behind it. Round 34 found that the 2010 worksheet's fixed 17 s Claremont Ave greens made things worse, and noted that the model codes Ashby Ave eastbound with one lane (OSM) where the worksheet has two. On 2026-11-16 the project lead relayed two resident-provided field observations: (a) Ashby Ave eastbound at Claremont Ave has two travel lanes in the PM peak; (b) Claremont Ave southbound at Ashby Ave has a green left arrow, as northbound does (observed 2026-11-15). With the worksheet in [SafewayCollege-DEIR2011], (b) means both Claremont Ave approaches run as split phases. The lead asked for the junction to be modeled from the observations, then the queue-first left-turn model, then the fit, keeping each step only if the validation suite and the network both improve or stay equal. The numbers are in round 35 of `data/calibration/rockridge.record.md`.

## Decisions

D1. Reproduction. `npm run data` in the round's worktree reproduced network 6a8285d5076f9ddc. The demand came out as 67eab02b55a59298, not bef51b684a8c079c: the only difference is one provenance URL (the Oakland multimodal count dataset), which commit debeb6c (bibliography deep links) changed in `build-demand.ts` after round 34 built its data. Flows, gates and every other field are identical, and PM seed 1 on the published files matches round 34's stored report in every field except the manifest (input paths and the area config hash).

D2. Phasing. The southbound observation enters `signalObservations` as an own phase for Claremont Ave southbound (heading 210), all periods, DATA for the phasing. The engine already ran both Claremont Ave approaches alone: an approach marked `ownPhase` leaves its street's phase, so with northbound marked the southbound approach was left alone in its own phase. The new entry changes no run (PM seed 1 identical except the list of observed approaches in the report). Cycle and greens follow the flows (Webster) on that structure, ASSUMPTION pending a timing sheet; Ashby Ave keeps one phase with permitted lefts, as in the worksheet. Kept: it records the observation and leaves every result equal.

D3. Ashby Ave eastbound lanes. The worksheet (PDF page 482) codes eastbound as one lane group of two lanes (lane utilization 0.95, saturated flow 3,490 veh/h) with its 99 left turns inside it, so no left-turn lane. OSM way 34361024 (checked 2024-03-15) is `lanes=2`, one each way, and the network had one eastbound lane (link 3395, 129 m from Claremont Crescent to the signal) with the class-rule pocket. An `osmTagOverrides` entry (`lanes=3`, `lanes:forward=2`, `lanes:backward=1`) gives the link two eastbound lanes: the whole link is the shortest extent the network holds without splitting a link, which would renumber links; the network has no lanes by period, so it applies to all periods (both ASSUMPTIONS). A lane-coding-only observation (D5) removed the class-rule pocket. The importer produced exactly the network measured on the scratch copy (same 4,411 links, no renumbering, only link 3395 changed).

D4. Step 1 reverted. PM cars cleared from gridlock per hour, seeds 1 to 10 / 11 to 20, round 34 against two lanes: shipped demand 1.1 / 1.3 against 2.4 / 1.7; iteration 2 13.9 / 13.6 against 12.6 / 13.6; iteration 3 10.6 / 16.0 against 14.5 / 11.2. Keeping the class-rule pocket (diagnostic) gave 3.6 / 3.1, 11.8 / 14.9 and 13.1 / 15.8. With two lanes the eastbound flow ratio per lane halves, so Webster gives the Ashby Ave phase less green (29.6 s of a 76.5 s cycle before, 23.7 s of 75 s after; means of the reported plans, shipped demand, seeds 1 to 10) and the Claremont Ave phases more; westbound then carries less (608 to 435 veh/h) and southbound more (437 to 561), eastbound about the same (474 to 480), and the gridlock moves onto the eastbound approach itself. The network got worse on the shipped demand on both seed sets, so the lane override and the lane coding were taken out of the area config. Ashby Ave westbound: the worksheet codes two lanes in one shared group (lane utilization 0.95, saturated flow 3,357 veh/h); OSM way 165864174 is `lanes=4` with no direction split and no `turn:lanes`, so the network already has two westbound lanes (link 3426, 37 m, too short for a pocket). They agree; nothing changed and no field check is needed for the count.

D5. Lane coding without a timing. The importer accepted `noLeftTurnLane` only on an observation with a timing or an own phase. An observation may now give the lane coding alone (no cycle, green, own phase, partner or clearance); the importer marks the approach `noPocket` and attaches no timing. A unit test covers it. It stays, as round 34's per-phase clearance did, because it changes nothing without the field and lets a lane coding from a timing sheet be entered as written.

D6. Queue-first left turns stay off. On the kept network, `--queue-first-left` gave shipped 1.6 / 2.6 against 1.1 / 1.3, iteration 2 16.1 / 17.1 against 13.9 / 13.6, iteration 3 13.8 / 20.2 against 10.6 / 16.0. Gridlock clearing got worse on every demand and seed set. On the two-lane network of step 1 (diagnostic) it gave 2.2 / 2.2, 13.3 / 20.4 and 14.5 / 14.9, also worse than round 34 on the shipped demand.

D7. Fit. With step 1 reverted and queue-first off, every run equals round 34's, so `tools/cost-loop.ts --no-stop --resume 3` replayed round 33's stored iterations under the unchanged acceptance rule: converged at iteration 3 (gap 0.090), every iteration rejected, and the final build gave the same flows. The shipped demand keeps its free-flow and BPR costs; its file differs from round 34's only in the provenance URL (D1) and the network hash it records.

D8. Verdict. Status stays `uncalibrated`. Every simulated figure equals round 34's (the assessment's seeds 1 to 10 of each period, and PM seeds 11 to 20 seed for seed); validation 61 of 72 as before. The area note is unchanged (still true at 156 characters); the caveat names both arrows and says, in visitor wording, that two eastbound lanes on Ashby Ave were tested and cleared more cars from gridlock, so the model keeps the map's one lane.
