# Count-constrained demand fit: rockridge

Generated by `tools/build-demand.ts` (do not edit). Car trips only, veh/h. Method and assumptions: `data/demand/README.md`, section "Count-constrained demand".

Settings: factor bounds 0.1 to 3, damping 1, tolerance 1.0% on the unblocked count stations, at most 600 iterations; flows that touch no count take the medianOfType factor (volume-weighted median); capacity-restrained assignment by successive averages (10 passes x 2 draws of perceived costs, BPR alpha 0.15 beta 4, route-choice dispersion 2 s, 2 round(s)); route-share constraints on, weight 3 (counts 1 to 2); capacity guard on (limit = capacity x 1.10, uncounted links only; an ASSUMPTION-labeled sanity rule, not data).

Candidates are the flows of the seed table whose route through the movement costs at most the detour limit times their best route; the volume of each intent is split over its candidates in proportion to their seed volume. Their volume is pinned in the fit. Plain flows of other pairs whose best route makes the turn give up volume until the turn carries no more than its count (the natural-flow line of each movement). Each flow carries the movement's detour tolerance and, where the movement has intents, the alternative movements of the same turn family (ASSUMPTION); the simulator keeps the movement only while its detour in the current network stays within the tolerance.

### Intents of the assumed movements (ASSUMPTION)

An intent names where the cars that make the movement come from and go to (approach groups of the area config). Its candidates are the candidate flows between those groups; its volume is split over them in proportion to their seed volume. The plain flow of each pair gives up the trips the assumed flow carries (at most 80% of it) before the fit, and the fit then rescales it to the counts.
