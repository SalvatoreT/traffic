## Context

Round 31 left the PM peak with too many southbound through cars on Hillegass Ave and Colby St and with Forest St eastbound at four times its count. The delay-feedback fit reaches the southbound ranges only by loading the arterials until the PM peak is unstable. The lead's reading is an arterial capacity deficit. This round locates it first, then tries lane use along a link, the closely spaced signals and the College Ave / Claremont Ave / 62nd St plan as separate measured steps, each kept only if the validation suite and the network both improve, and then refits the demand. The numbers are in round 32 of `data/calibration/rockridge.record.md`; this file records the decisions.

## Decisions

D1. Locate before changing. The delay-feedback demand of round 31 at iteration 2 was rebuilt from the stored iteration 0 and 1 runs into a scratch directory (`build-demand.ts --out-root`, a copy of the config pointing at a scratch delays file); its PM seed 1 reproduced the stored run exactly. `calibrate --node-report` (new, changes no run) writes per-link exits, standing vehicle-seconds, the share of 5 s samples near jam density, the mean signal plans and the removals. On PM seeds 1 to 4 with `--od-costs`, each location is ranked by the through cars on the counted side-street blocks whose arterial alternative it slows by more than the margin by which the side street won. Capacity is lanes x s x g/C with s from 1,500 to 2,000 veh/h per lane [FHWA-SIG2004] and the model's mean green share.

D2. What the diagnosis says. No arterial approach on the through cars' alternatives reaches its saturation-flow capacity on that demand: the Telegraph Ave approaches at Aileen St, 56th St, 55th St, 52nd St and Alcatraz Ave run at 0.3 to 0.65 of it, and College Ave southbound at Claremont Ave at about 0.4 to 0.5. Their throughput is below the counts (Telegraph Ave at 55th St 944 and 870 against 1,070 and 1,099) because less demand reaches them, not because they cap it. What they impose is delay: four uncoordinated signals within 300 m on Telegraph Ave, and left-turners that block a lane. The removals sit elsewhere: the Claremont Ave northbound approach to Ashby Ave (314 veh/h against 577 counted, 9 vehicles standing, a two-lane approach with heavy left turns) and the hillside streets that feed it (Claremont Blvd at Avalon Ave, Claremont Crescent, Russell St), the Harmon St circles, Martin Luther King Jr Way at 47th St and 52nd St at Telegraph Ave. The deficit, then, is delay at closely spaced signals and lane blocking, which steps 2 and 3 address; a shortfall of signal capacity was not found.

D3. Lane changes along a link, kept. MOBIL [Kesting2007] with the equations and values read in Kesting, Treiber and Helbing (arXiv 1012.4913, eqs. 4 and 5) and on Treiber's MOBIL page (b_safe 4 m/s^2, threshold 0.2 m/s^2, politeness realistic in 0 to 0.5, a minimum time between changes). ASSUMPTIONS: politeness 0.2, 3 s between changes of a driver, one check a second, 1 m minimum gap, a missing leader counts as a standing vehicle at the stop line in both lanes compared, a forced change (lane cannot serve the next movement, or a left-turner at a pocket with room) needs only the safety criterion, and discretionary changes stay on the side of the road the driver sorts for. Changes are instantaneous and run before the heads are evaluated, so a car that becomes a lane's head is judged in the same step. Validation: b9 rose from 814 to 870 veh/h per lane (range 815 to 1,075), the new case l2 passes (1.04 of the offered flow; 4 vehicles left behind the obstruction against 59 without lane changes), the new case l1 fails (792 against 851 to 1,064; 699 without) because a left-turner that does not fit the pocket still holds the inner lane at the stop line. l1 is listed in `validation.deviations.json` as not reviewed, for the lead. Network, PM shipped demand, seeds 1 to 20 against round 31: removals 1.7 against 1.55 an hour (inside the seed spread), headline error 8.7 and 7.0 against 8.9 and 7.7, fitted GEH share up 2 points, Forest St eastbound 237 and 247 against 275 and 290, Telegraph Ave southbound 334 and 341 s against 357 and 360. On the delay-feedback demand removals 4.9 against 5.4 an hour and Telegraph Ave southbound 337 against 361 s.

D4. Closely spaced signals, kept. Why round 29's attempt failed, as far as can be said without its code: it joined every signal along a street within 800 m (then 150 m) on the longest member cycle, which lengthens the queues on 81 to 94 m links (the Signal Timing Manual's own remedy for queues on short links is shorter cycles, [FHWA-STM2008] 6.7.8), and it used simultaneous offsets, which the manual recommends for oversaturated arterials (6.4.3) while D2 shows these approaches undersaturated, where the manual sets offsets so a platoon released at the upstream start of green arrives at the downstream start of green (6.6.4). The rule here: signals joined by at most 120 m of road (ASSUMPTION; a link, or links going straight on through uncontrolled nodes) run the longest of their own cycles (6.3.1, 6.6.2) and the downstream green for the joining road starts one free-flow travel time after the upstream green of the approach feeding it straight on, in the direction with the larger measured approach flow; at each adaptation the new plans keep the common cycle and offsets and start no earlier than the end of each signal's running cycle. The first version paired only single links; it left 55th St out of the interchange group (node 700 lies between 55th St and 56th St) and 55th St westbound produced 10 removals over seeds 11 to 20; the version through uncontrolled nodes has none there. Queue-aware gating of the upstream green was not built: with the offsets the removals at the interchange are gone. Validation: c1 and c2 rise from 0.78 and 0.76 to 1.03 and 1.03 (the notes give the uncoordinated values); c3 and c4 (200 m) are unchanged. Network, PM shipped demand, seeds 1 to 20, lane changes and coordination: removals 0.8 and 1.4 an hour (1.1 against 1.55), headline error 7.1 and 5.7, through cars Hillegass Ave SB 137 and 142, Colby St SB 128 and 142, Forest St eastbound 192 and 208, Telegraph Ave southbound 311 and 320 s.

D5. College Ave / Claremont Ave / 62nd St observed plan, not kept. The 2010 PM worksheet [SafewayCollege-DEIR2011] (the four observations of round 29, applied to a scratch network) on the engine of D3 and D4, PM seeds 1 to 20: removals 1.4 and 1.8 an hour (1.6 against 1.1 without it), 8 of them at Ashby Ave / Claremont Ave; headline error 6.0 and 4.9 with all four shares within 10 points; southbound through cars 134 and 139 on Hillegass Ave, 125 and 131 on Colby St; northbound rose above their ranges (Hillegass Ave 111 and 108, Colby St 161 and 157); 62nd St eastbound 94 and 102. Stability got worse, so under the step rule the plan stays out of the area config.
