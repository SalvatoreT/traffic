## Context

Round 37 judged gridlock near the Trapezoid and replayed the delay-feedback fit. Every iteration stayed rejected on entry backlog, and the backlog readings note set out what a near-only backlog check would change. Times are Pacific; the round ran on 2026-11-16.

## Decisions

D1. Decisions by the project lead, 2026-11-16 ("Keep the current model"). The delay-feedback refit is not adopted, and the entry-backlog check of D16 stays network-wide. The refit reached the cut-through ranges partly by sending too little traffic toward the study area (Telegraph Ave at Alcatraz Ave, north leg: 763 veh/h against 1,064 counted) and by leaving 370 to 460 trips waiting at far entries. The published model stays the shipped demand 1c798217e72fb1ba. This round goes after two measurable errors in that model, the excess on Telegraph Ave and the trips waiting at the SR-24 mainline gate. A change is kept only if validation and the network both improve; through-car ranges and held-out stations are checks, not targets.

D2. Reproduction and resources. `npm run data` in the round's worktree reproduced network f9fa09a5726644d7, demand 1c798217e72fb1ba and cross-validation demand a367850a13850985 byte for byte. The container restarted during the round as well. A demand build run alone, with its memory sampled every second, peaked at 1.8 to 1.9 GB of resident memory (shipped and training views, with and without measured delays), and the scratch reassignment of D4 at 0.5 GB, against 16 GB. The builds are not what exhausts memory.

D3. Where the Telegraph Ave excess comes from. Ten-seed PM flows (seeds 1 to 10) against the fit's assigned flows, link by link:
- Telegraph Ave southbound agrees from the north edge to Ashby Ave (697 assigned, 665 simulated). The excess starts at Ashby Ave: past it the fit has 476 and the simulation 628. Ashby Ave westbound brings 466 into the junction against 337 assigned, and turns left onto Telegraph Ave. A further 94 come in from 66th St eastbound (131 against 37), and the excess reaches 228 at Alcatraz Ave (1,307 against 1,080).
- At Alcatraz Ave, the approach from the west (W leg, counted 547) carries 285 simulated against 569 assigned. Alcatraz Ave eastbound is one lane between Shattuck Ave and Telegraph Ave. The simulated mean delay per vehicle is 35 s on the link into Shattuck Ave, 32 s into Irwin Ct and 33 s into Telegraph Ave; the fit's costs have none of it.
- The excess stays on Telegraph Ave southbound and leaves it at the SR-24 eastbound on-ramp at 56th St: 979 simulated, 789 assigned, 657 counted. The Claremont Ave on-ramp at Hudson St also takes more (853, 727 and 598). Eastbound on-ramps farther east take less (Broadway, 219 against 359). Ashby Ave eastbound toward Claremont Ave and Tunnel Rd carries 474 against 975 assigned, with 69 to 76 s of delay per vehicle at Claremont Ave and at Domingo Ave / Tunnel Rd, the chain of round 36.
- Checks: with live guidance off (`--guided-share 0`, PM seeds 1 to 3) the north leg carries 1,350 (1,321 with it), so en-route switching is not the cause. With signal coordination off (`--coord-max-m 0`) it carries 1,271, so coordination explains about 50 of the 240 (cause c). No assumed movement (`via`) is on Telegraph Ave: those of the volunteer count are about 280 veh/h of turns inside the study area, and the large `via` volumes are the off-ramp choices on SR-24 (cause b).

D4. The zone-only cure is not justified. Delays only in and around the judged zone reproduce the simulation worse than the current costs at every scale tried (RMSE 199 and 203 against 169; at Alcatraz Ave W leg 56 to 58 against 285). The congestion that moves these trips lies on Alcatraz Ave west of Telegraph Ave, Ashby Ave east of College Ave and the SR-24 ramps, so a zone restricted to the study area cannot carry it. It was not built.

D5. Damped feedback (tested, not kept). Method of successive averages with step 1/n [PowellSheffi1982]: taking the free-flow costs as the first iterate and the simulated delays as the second gives half of the measured delay. That is also the weight that reproduced the simulation in D3. The demand fit gained `countFit.assignment.measured.weight` (default 1, so the shipped build is unchanged). A demand was built with weight 0.5, BPR alpha 0.15 kept, and the ten-seed delays of the shipped demand in all three periods. PM results, shipped demand in brackets. Seeds 1 to 10: the Telegraph Ave north leg at Alcatraz Ave carries 1,210 (1,307), the W leg 352 (285), the 56th St on-ramp 896 (979). Through cars HSB / CSB / HNB / CNB are 138 / 134 / 94 / 154 (142 / 132 / 97 / 150), headline error 5.5 with 4 of 4 shares within 10 points (7.5, 3 of 4). But cars cleared from gridlock rise to 17.4 an hour (1.1), 17.3 of them far from the study area: Avalon Ave / Claremont Blvd, Ashby Ave at Claremont Crescent and at Claremont Ave, Martin Luther King Jr Way at 61st and 62nd St. Arterial speed falls to 7.27 m/s (8.26), and recent fitted stations under GEH 5 drop to 70 of 92 (75). Seeds 11 to 20: north leg 1,191 (1,368), headline error 7.0 with 3 of 4 (6.0, 3 of 4), cleared 15.2 an hour (1.3), fitted stations 70 of 92 (75). The judged count stays under 1 on both sets and backlog passes. The network got worse on both seed sets (far gridlock, speed, fitted stations), and the error went from 23% to 13% over the count, so the step is not kept. The option stays in the code with the default weight 1, and the shipped build does not use it.

D6. The SR-24 mainline gate. SR-24 mainline gate (node 602): the waiting comes from the first off-ramps, not from inserting vehicles onto the mainline.
- The gate has two first links: the four-lane mainline (link 1447, 56 m) and a three-lane collector (link 4167, 75 m). The collector forks into the 52nd St off-ramp (link 4160, two lanes) and the Martin Luther King Jr Way off-ramp (link 4175, two lanes, then link 4161, one lane: a lane drop). The mainline link has no delay (0.1 s per vehicle) and passes everything offered to it.
- The demand sends 1,630 veh/h to the Martin Luther King Jr Way off-ramp and 1,009 to the 52nd St one (ramp choice `via`). The simulation passes 1,516 and 925 (counted 1,614 and 1,024). The two-lane approach to the lane drop has 57 s of delay per vehicle. Validation case i5 puts the model's 2-to-1 lane drop at 0.81 of a lane's flow (1,542 veh/h), below the 1,614 counted on that ramp, so most of the queue is a capacity limit of the model's lane drop, which stays within i5's assumed range.
- The backlog starts growing after minute 25, when that queue reaches the gate. Waiting trips leave a gate in order per first link, so trips for the 52nd St ramp wait behind trips for the lane drop, although they use other lanes of the collector. That part is an insertion artifact.
- Validation: h3 (new) shows a 3-lane gate link admits 1,922 veh/h per lane, its lane capacity. h4 (new) measures the artifact: a gate onto a 3-lane fork, with one branch queued back to the gate, passes 0.43 of the free branch's flow against the same network with the other branch unqueued (range 0.90 to 1.10, ASSUMPTION). It fails, and is listed as a deviation, not reviewed.
- Engine fix tested, not landed: a waiting trip waits only behind trips for the same fork branch (`fork_band` of the movement from its first link to its next), so other trips may pass it. Fingerprint ba5e76c11ec1 instead of f0df1aabc323. A first version keyed on the next link changed the signal cases b4 and b9 (passing on single-lane approaches by driver type) and was dropped. Keyed on the fork branch, every other case is unchanged and h4 rises to 0.64. On PM seeds 1 to 10 and 11 to 20, trips waiting at the gate fall from 143 to 84 per seed (to 68 on seeds 11 to 20). Total backlog falls from 0.71% to 0.52% and from 0.57% to 0.44%, and the 52nd St off-ramp carries 990 and 996 against 1,024 counted (925 and 952). The study area does not change: north leg 1,309 and 1,376, through cars 143 / 139 / 97 / 151 and 146 / 144 / 97 / 151, recent fitted stations 75 and 74 of 92 (75 and 75), mean fitted GEH 3.83 and 3.78 (3.73 and 3.76). The gain is real but sits outside the study area, the station fit does not improve, and the change would mark every published proposal out of date. So it was not landed; the patch is kept with the round's scratch output. Repairing the lane drop as well (i5) would be the matching change, if the lead wants the SR-24 entry fixed.

D7. Evaluation. Evaluation. The shipped model is unchanged: demand 1c798217e72fb1ba, network f9fa09a5726644d7, simulator f0df1aabc323. Only `validate.rs` (outside the fingerprint) gained h3 and h4, so validation reads 62 of 74 (61 of 72 plus h3 passing and h4 failing). Every simulated figure equals round 37's. No rule, range or target changed, no deviation was marked reviewed, and the status stays `uncalibrated`. Neither step gave an improvement that holds on both seed sets. What would settle the Telegraph Ave error is data on the delays that move the trips: a PM turning-movement count with queue observations at Alcatraz Ave / Shattuck Ave and Alcatraz Ave / Telegraph Ave (west approach), a count of the SR-24 eastbound on-ramp at Telegraph Ave / 56th St (the 2012 Caltrans figure is held out), and the counts at Claremont Ave / Russell St named in round 36.
