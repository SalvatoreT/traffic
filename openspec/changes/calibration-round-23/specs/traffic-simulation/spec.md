## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: An observed approach can have green alone and then with the opposing approach
When the observed timing of a signal marks an approach with the approaches it also runs with, the plan SHALL give that approach a phase of its own whose green is its observed through green less the green of the partner, and SHALL add the approach to the partner's phase, so the approach has its whole through green and the partner keeps its own [FHWA-STM2008]. A link in two phases SHALL show the better of the two signal states. This represents the lead-lag of a worksheet (a protected left that leads the opposing through), as an assumption.

#### Scenario: Eastbound through 57.2 s, westbound 27.6 s
- **WHEN** an approach with 57.2 s of green runs with an opposing approach of 27.6 s in a 110 s cycle
- **THEN** the plan has three phases, the first with the approach alone for 29.6 s, the second with both for 27.6 s and the third with the cross street, and the approach has green for 57.2 s of the cycle
