## Context

Rounds 36 to 38 and the compact-extent model did not bring the through cars on the four counted blocks into their ranges. PM seeds 1 to 10 (11 to 20), shipped demand 1c798217e72fb1ba: Hillegass Ave SB 143 (146), Colby St SB 132 (140), Hillegass Ave NB 98 (96), Colby St NB 150 (152), against 79 to 120, 39 to 117, 48 to 95 and 102 to 145. Times are Pacific; the round ran on 2026-11-17.

## Decisions

D1. Decision by the project lead, 2026-11-16 (verbatim choice: "Yes, fit to the cut-through counts"). The four counted through-car figures (Hillegass Ave and Colby St, each direction, PM peak, from the resident volunteer count of Feb 25, 2025) become targets of the demand fit, like the other counts. The app exists to give cut-through numbers for these streets. The cost, stated on the site and in the record: these four figures stop being an independent check. The independent checks left are the held-out count stations, the headline freeway shares, stability and the arterial travel times.

D2. The compact model is shelved. Its second round (cordon prior from the current model) failed the lead's stop rule: southbound through cars stayed at 200 to 231 an hour and the fitted evidence got worse (`rockridge.extent-comparison.md`). `rockridge-compact` stays in the repository on its as-built demand and is not published as a candidate.

D3. What the four figures are. The volunteer count gives, per street and direction (PM, `routeDiagrams` table totals and the block maps):
- whole-street cars: cars that drove the street from one end to the other (Hillegass Ave NB 48, Colby St NB 102, Hillegass Ave SB 79, Colby St SB 39);
- end-along cars: cars whose trip ended along the street (15, 29, 40, 43);
- the block volume (`observedVehPerHour`, the median of the street's counted blocks: 110, 174, 160, 160).

A through car has neither trip end in the study area. Whole-street cars are through cars, so they bound the figure from below; some through cars turn on or off partway, which the count does not follow. The block volume less the end-along cars bounds it from above: it still holds cars that start along the street or end elsewhere in the study area. Hence a range, not a value: 48 to 95, 102 to 145, 79 to 120, 39 to 117 (the calibrate report's `throughCheck` since round 19). The count says nothing about where in the range the true figure lies, so the fit target is the middle of the range (71.5, 123.5, 99.5, 78; ASSUMPTION) and the range is its tolerance.

D4. What the count already gives the fit on these streets, so nothing is counted twice:
- Block volumes on every counted block (directed link stations; every fourth held out).
- Whole-street route counts (`count route ...`): all cars, through or not, that drive the street end to end, the lower ends of the ranges above. They constrain path shares in the fit.
- Turning movements at the street ends (Alcatraz Ave and Claremont Ave turn-ins and turn-outs) as movement stations.
- Assumed movement flows (`assumedMovementFlows`, round 20): pinned flows that make the Alcatraz Ave turn-ins into Hillegass Ave and Colby St southbound and the turn-outs northbound. About 133 through trips an hour (PM) are pinned on the four streets.
- The StreetLight select-link shares (freeway share of the block's trips) as ratio constraints and the volumes they imply (DERIVED, weight 0.5).

The through target is a different quantity from each of these (a through-only, any-link count of one street run), so none of them is a copy of it. The pinned assumed flows are part of the through figure: they stay pinned, their through trips count against the target, and only the plain flows move.

D5. Mechanisms weighed.
- (a) Static select-link through targets only: a constraint on the share of each through flow's assigned routes that use the street run. Cheap and generic, but rounds 30 and 37 showed the static assignment puts fewer through cars on these streets than the simulator does (its drivers take the side streets on routes the static costs rank worse: perceived-cost noise per node, delays the fit cannot see). A target that the static fit already meets moves nothing.
- (b) Simulation-in-the-loop correction outside the fit: after a fit, scale the through demand of the origin-destination groups that the simulator puts on these streets by a damped multiplicative factor, iterate. It acts on what the simulator does, but scaling flows outside the fit moves every count those flows cross (gate counts, Telegraph Ave, the blocks), so a second balancing step would be needed, and the groups are a choice.
- (c) Chosen: the target sits inside the fit (a), and its membership comes from the simulator (b). Each through flow's proportion on a street run is its pair's share of simulated trips counted on that run over ten seeds (`calibrate --select-od`), so the fit scales the pairs that the simulator actually sends there, while every other count, gate counts included, stays a constraint in the same fit and balances the change. The loop repeats: build, simulate ten seeds, pool the counted trips of all iterations (method of successive averages, step 1/n, [PowellSheffi1982]), build again. No step touches the simulator engine, so the fingerprint stays f0df1aabc323.

Definition match. The simulator's figure (`throughCheck.modeledThrough`) counts each vehicle once per street run when any of its links is in the vehicle's trip (completed trips and vehicles still in the network that have used it), with neither trip end at a study-area node, per hour of the 60-minute run. The fit's static membership uses a new route feature (`any`: the route uses any link of the run, counted once) restricted to flows with neither end in the study area, on the same links (`resolveBlock`, checked equal to the calibrate report's links). The measured membership is computed from the same select-link records as the report; pooled over the ten baseline seeds it reproduces the report's means exactly (97.5, 149.8, 142.5, 132.1).

Membership estimate (ASSUMPTION). For a plain flow of pair (o, d): (trips counted on the run − trips of the pair's pinned flows on it) / (trips the pair's plain flows offered over the runs), with the static route share as a prior worth 5 trips. A pair with no counted trip in any run takes the prior against its offered volume in this table. The same estimate serves every flow of the pair; the via-split of off-ramp flows happens after the fit.

Where the trips taken off these streets go. They are removed from the trip table, not rerouted. The pairs are gravity-seed estimates (ASSUMPTION), not counts, while the through figure is data. Forcing them onto the arterial with the same ends would need a route constraint (a `via` movement) that no count supports. The fit's gate stations and arterial counts stay in force, so at counted gates the fit restores the gate total from other pairs, and at assumed (class-default) gates the volume simply falls. The record reports the change by gate and by origin-destination group.

Generic form. Any select-link block of an area's targets file marked `fitThrough` becomes a target (`countFit.throughTargets` in the demand config: weight, measured-membership file, prior, treatment of the other periods). The ranges come from the same fields the calibrate report already reads. Nothing names a street in code.

D6. Other periods (ASSUMPTION). AM and midday have no through-car counts. The PM range times the period's volume factor (`periodFactors`: AM 0.85, midday 0.62, the convention the build already uses for derived targets) becomes a one-sided limit: the target acts only while that period's figure is above the scaled upper end, and then pulls to the scaled middle. It is a derived target and is not scored. AM membership comes from AM seed runs, midday from midday runs, both on the baseline demand. The AM peak runs mostly the other way on these streets (whole-street cars AM 34, 33, 36, 46), so a two-sided scaled target would push AM through traffic up with no data behind it; the one-sided limit only keeps the PM correction from being undone where the periods share pairs.

D7. Training view. The four street runs each hold a held-out block of the frozen split (Colby St 2593/2594/2601/2602, Hillegass Ave 2673/2674), so, by the rule that already leaves the whole-street route counts out of the training view, the through targets are left out of it too. The cross-validation demand is therefore unchanged by construction, and the held-out stations are scored out of sample on it as before. The record also reports the held-out stations on the shipped demand's runs, marked in sample.

D8. Guard rails, frozen before any result of the new demand was seen. Baseline = the shipped demand on the same seeds (round 38). PM is judged on seeds 1 to 10 and on 11 to 20; each rail must hold on both.
- G1 Station fit: recent fitted stations within GEH 5, no more than 2 fewer than the baseline (75 of 92 on both PM sets; AM seeds 1 to 10: 67 of 77).
- G2 Headline freeway shares (D5 of the baseline design): no fewer shares within 10 points than the baseline (3 of 4 on both sets), and the mean absolute error no more than 1.5 points above it (7.5 and 6.0).
- G3 Stability (D16, unchanged rules): judged near-zone clearings below 1 an hour, backlog under 2% in at least 9 of 10 seeds, no runaway growth, arterial speed above 4 m/s; and the network-wide mean entry backlog no more than 0.15 points above the baseline (0.71% and 0.57%). Far clearings are reported.
- G4 Telegraph Ave travel times (ACTC ranges): PM northbound and southbound stay inside 302 to 420 s and 300 to 376 s; AM northbound stays inside 280 to 315 s and AM southbound (299 s, already above 245 to 285) rises by no more than 10 s.
- G5 Telegraph Ave / Alcatraz Ave counted legs (N entering 1,064, W entering 547, S leaving 910, E leaving 689): on each leg the GEH rises by no more than 1.5 over the baseline of the same seed set.
- Reported, not rails: held-out stations (D7), AM and midday through figures and everything else in the assessment, the far gridlock, entry backlog by gate.

D9. Stopping rule, frozen with D8. Iterate (build, PM seeds 1 to 10, pool, rebuild) until all four simulated figures are inside their ranges on seeds 1 to 10; then run seeds 11 to 20. Stop when all four are inside on both sets, or when an iteration does not cut the summed distance outside the ranges (seeds 1 to 10) by at least 10%, or after four iterations. Take the iteration that is inside on both sets (or, failing that, the one with the smallest summed distance) and stop; the central values are not chased past the ranges. If that iteration breaks a guard rail, try weight 0.5 on the targets once; if that also breaks one, the shipped demand stays and the record says so.
