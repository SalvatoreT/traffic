P1. (Preparation choice.) The nodes that count are those strictly between the run's ends: the heads of the run's links that are also the tail of another link of the run (`interiorNodes`, `tools/lib/share-targets.ts`). Colby Street, both directions: 786, 793, 799, 819, 828, 1070, 1071 (run ends 853 at Alcatraz Avenue and 1069 at Claremont Avenue). Hillegass Avenue, both directions: 1085, 1086, 1090 (ends 1110 and 798). Why: the count's three categories partition the cars that enter at the top (turned off at a cross street, ended along the street, drove the whole street). The simulator counts a whole-street trip when a car has driven every link of the run in order, from the first link's start to the last link's stop line (`crates/sim/src/sim/corridor.rs`). A trip that ends at the run's last node has driven every link, so it is a whole-street candidate in the count's sense; counting it as end-along too would pull the two figures against each other. A trip cannot end at the first node and have used the run. Whether the simulator's whole-street observer sees a trip that ends at the last node was not checked here (no simulation); excluding the node keeps the categories apart either way. The lead's text allowed the last node; this is the choice to review. It is one line: `interiorNodes`.

P2. (Preparation choice.) Where the constraint lives in the fit: in the station-level set `counts`, so it enters both the count-only fit and the final fit (as the through targets and the whole-street route stations do), not only the second fit as the shares do. Flows that touch it count as counted for the median rule and the capacity guard's cut, like any other count. The report's "fitted stations within tolerance" does not count it.

P3. (Preparation choice.) "Static routes when no membership is in use (pass 0, midday)" is read narrowly. Inside such a period a run with no simulated trip takes static routes (its share is the static fraction of the flow's sampled routes that use a link of the run, the denominator of the select-link share) and the report says `static`. The static fit that anchors the ceilings has no membership, so it has no constraint. If the lead wants the static-route version in pass 0, the change is the gate `endOn` in `fitPeriod`, and pass 0 would then no longer reproduce the baseline periods.

## What is not known

- Whether the fit can reach the end-along figures. It scales existing pairs, at most 10 for local flows and 3 for through flows, so a node with almost no destination volume in the seed table cannot be lifted to its figure. The published PM Colby Street destinations at 819 and 1071 are 0.7 an hour each, so reaching a share of the 43 there would need a factor above 10; most of the volume sits at McAuley Street (828, 29 an hour) and Forest Street side nodes. The checks below report the seed volumes.
- Whether destinations at the intersection nodes are ends along the street. A cross street also reaches 819 or 1071, and a trip from a cross street that ends at the intersection has not "ended along" Colby Street in the count's sense. The membership counts only trips that used a link of the run, which removes the pairs that never touch it, but a trip that enters the run for one block and ends at its next intersection counts although a volunteer might have seen it turn off.
- Whether the end-along constraint raises the number of local trip ends enough to trigger the 3% abort. More local ends in the study area raise offered demand; round 52's PM shipped build offers 10.3% less than the static fit, so there is room, but the loop's abort above +3% stays.
- Whether the three constraints on Colby Street SB (block counts, whole-street count, end-along count) can all be met at once by the pairs the table has. The counts agree (139 = 39 + 57 + 43) but the table's pairs may not.
- How the held-out counts move: they cannot (P4), and the shipped-view fit moves the interior streets that the held stations sit on only through the demand the cross-validation build never sees.

## Risks

- The 43 cars may come from pairs that the table has few of, so the fit raises their flows to the bound and still falls short; the end-along figure would then stay low and the whole-street figure may not fall by 15.
- Flows that end at interior nodes rise, and so do the interior streets' loads (Colby Street, Hillegass Avenue and the cross streets near their ends). Backlog may rise (round 52 PM ended at 1.07% mean backlog, under the 2% line) and so may gridlock clearances in the judged zone.
- The weight 2 and the node rule are assumptions with no reference. A volunteer count over one peak hour is noisy; the fit now trusts eight more figures.
- Destinations that rise at an intersection node also feed the cross streets' block counts, which are fitted stations or held-out stations; a held-out cross street may move through the shipped fit only, and the held-out rows are read on the cross-validation demand, which does not change.
- The loop has not run with the new report lines. A mismatch stops the loop at the check.
