# Count-constrained demand fit: rockridge

Generated by `tools/build-demand.ts` (do not edit). Car trips only, veh/h. Method and assumptions: `data/demand/README.md`, section "Count-constrained demand".

Settings: factor bounds 0.1 to 3, damping 1, tolerance 1.0% on the unblocked count stations, at most 600 iterations; flows that touch no count take the medianOfType factor (volume-weighted median); capacity-restrained assignment by successive averages (10 passes x 2 driver walks, BPR alpha 0.15 beta 4, walk noise +/-8%, 2 round(s)); route-share constraints on, weight 3 (counts 1 to 2); capacity guard on (limit = capacity x 1.10, uncounted links only; an ASSUMPTION-labeled sanity rule, not data).
