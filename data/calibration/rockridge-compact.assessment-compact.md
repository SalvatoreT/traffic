### What the held-out figures show, and what they do not

The held-out scores below are a **diagnostic holdout**, not independent validation. The held stations are those of the frozen split `251dc047ca31`: whole dependency groups (every derivative of a withheld source observation, both directions and every period), chosen from station ids and corridors without reading any count value, and their observations are removed from the cross-validation demand's gate totals, ramp volumes, trip ends, assumed movements and fit.

- Earlier calibration rounds chose demand settings while reading station-level scores, held-out ones included (`data/calibration/rockridge.record.md`). Splitting the same observations again produces a useful diagnostic but cannot undo that history, and parameters tuned in those rounds (rates, factors, class defaults, weights) were not re-chosen without the held observations.
- Independent validation needs a frozen model and observations that no calibration round consulted (new counts, or other days), or an outer evaluation split that contains the whole model-selection procedure [WebTAG-M3.1] appendix C. A count from one day does not establish performance on other days [FHWA-TAT3-2019]; most recent stations here come from one counting day (see the source line in the verdict).
- Evidence that stays shared and is reported, not removed: the legs and movements of one intersection are not independent rows (flow conservation); whole-street counts that cover a held block are left out of the cross-validation demand and are not scored, but a modeled route can still use detours off its street, which a static rule cannot list.
- Fewer than 10 recent held-out stations are too few to judge; the population is not enlarged or filtered after seeing results.
- The split holds 59 of 254 dependency groups (64 stations). Aggregate counts left out of the cross-validation demand because they cover a held block: count move 318>256 (blocks 256); count route 591>605 (blocks 593, 594, 601, 602); count route 606>592 (blocks 593, 594, 601, 602); count route 669>675 (blocks 673, 674); count route 676>670 (blocks 673, 674). Correlated evidence the split cannot remove: Legs and movements of one intersection obey flow conservation (their published volumes are not independent rows), and a whole-street route can follow detours off its street; neither is a shared source row, so the split does not group them. Several observations were taken on one day or in one environmental study; evidence from one counting day does not establish performance on other days [FHWA-TAT3-2019].
