### Residents' detour

The least-detour objective takes, among the packages that remove at least 50% of the most removed (ASSUMPTION: the share), the one with the smallest added elapsed time per requested trip through the run cutoff. All eligible requests count from departure until completion or the cutoff, including any still waiting, removed or unroutable. The cohort excludes trips requested or placed before time zero. A difference in detour the paired comparison cannot resolve goes to the package that removes more through cars, then to the one with fewer changes.
