### Residents' detour

The least-detour objective takes, among the packages that remove at least 50% of the most removed (ASSUMPTION: the share), the one with the smallest added resident trip time per trip. A difference in detour the paired comparison cannot resolve goes to the package that removes more through cars, then to the one with fewer changes.
